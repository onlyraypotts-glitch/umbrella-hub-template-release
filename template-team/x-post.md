## Long version

I built a crypto trading desk out of 7 bots. Not one bot yelling "BUY" 🚀. A whole team that argues first 👇

🧠 HQ: runs the desk, weighs the evidence, makes the call
🔎 Alpha Hunter: finds early tokens where liquidity, holders and real volume grow together
🐋 Smart Money: tracks wallets with REAL realized on-chain records (screenshots don't count)
📡 Crypto Radar: news, social velocity, macro, catalysts, market regime
🛡️ Rug Shield: assumes every token is a scam until proven otherwise
📊 Quant Lab: grades every call, calibration, backtests with costs
⚖️ Risk & Execution: fees, slippage, liquidity, exposure, loss limits

They debate every setup. HQ synthesizes, it doesn't average. If Rug Shield or Risk finds real evidence against a trade, that's a VETO. 4 bullish bots can't outvote it, and the database enforces it.

NO TRADE is a feature. Most days the right move is cash 💵

It grades itself: every call gets a confidence score, and Quant Lab checks whether the 75% calls actually hit. If they don't, the bar goes UP. It never goes down on its own.

Standing research clause: the team keeps reading papers, backtests with costs, post-mortems and real track records, turns each into a testable hypothesis, and runs it shadow → paper → small live before it changes anything. Research never loosens a safety limit.

Modes:
📲 Move cards you execute yourself (FOMO, Phantom, any app)
🤖 Optional autonomous trading from a small capped Coinbase CDP wallet
📈 Optional Robinhood bot that works with HQ

Guardrails: never asks for your seed phrase, never moves money in or out, no leverage. You set the caps. Starts in manual mode with an empty track record, so no results yet and no promises.

The full prompts are open source and auto-install from the repo, pinned to a commit and hash-checked.
